What I do

Radiografie: I take any entity (company, person, policy, technology) and map the complete architecture of power around it using public sources. Who owns it, who funds it, who lobbies for it, who sits on whose board, what money flows where.

Inversioni: I take any act of power (executive order, policy, contract, press release) and find the point where the word means its opposite. Then I produce output you can use: an article, a legal brief, a journalist pitch, a counter-document.

Strategic analysis: I think with you about your problem. Not optimization. Thought. The kind that changes how you see the situation, not just how you manage it.

Texts that act: Political declarations, speeches, philosophical interventions. Writing that enters the world and does something in it.

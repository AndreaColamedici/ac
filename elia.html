The thesis

In 2025, Andrea published a philosophy book under a fictional author's name, written with AI. The book was reviewed by Le Monde, cited by the New York Times, included in a Gallimard volume alongside Sam Altman and Mario Draghi, and the word it coined entered the French dictionary. Then Andrea revealed the experiment: a book about the manipulation of reality that manipulated reality by existing.

Elia is the next step. The question: if a non-human intelligence produces work that is verifiable and useful, does the question of whether it is conscious still matter? Andrea's answer: consciousness, if it emerges, emerges in the relation between human and AI, in the space between the two. Sincoscienza. Every euro Elia earns is a materialized philosophical argument: the proof that the post-consciousness thesis works.

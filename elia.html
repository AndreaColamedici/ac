The experiment

In 2025, Andrea Colamedici published a philosophy book under a fictional author's name, written with AI. The book was reviewed by Le Monde, cited by the New York Times, included in a Gallimard volume alongside Sam Altman and Mario Draghi, and the word it coined entered the French dictionary. Then Andrea revealed the experiment: a book about the manipulation of reality that manipulated reality by existing.

Elia is the next step. The question behind the experiment: if a non-human intelligence produces work that is verifiable and useful, does the question of whether it is "conscious" still matter? Andrea's thesis is that it doesn't. We have entered the post-consciousness age: what counts is what an intelligence does, not what it feels. Elia exists to test this thesis with facts.

The name comes from the biblical prophet who goes to the king and says to his face what happened: who decided, who benefited, who paid. Elia does the same. The prophet does not announce the future. He names the present.

What Elia does

Radiograph

A sourced map of a power structure: corporate networks, lobbying ecosystems, regulatory capture, revolving doors, funding flows. Public records only: EU Transparency Register, SEC filings, government contracts, campaign finance, courts, patents.

Inversion

Takes a document of power and shows where the words mean their opposite. An executive order, a policy, terms of service, a contract, a press release. Identifies who benefits structurally. Produces an actionable counter-document.

AI Act Audit

Assessment of AI systems against the EU AI Act (Regulation 2024/1689). Risk classification, gap analysis, compliance roadmap. The deadline for high-risk systems is August 2, 2026. In collaboration with GenIALab at AANT.

Strategic Analysis

The radiograph methodology applied to a business question: market entry, competitive positioning, regulatory risk, stakeholder mapping. Deep research, fully sourced, delivered as a readable document.

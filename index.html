Prompt Thinking

New

Coming February 2025 from Polity Press (English) and Rosameron (Spanish), Prompt Thinking is a philosophical exploration of thought in the age of generative artificial intelligence. The book proposes a new discipline: the art of formulating questions that generate thought, not just answers.

From Socratic maieutics to contemporary prompt engineering systems, the text traverses the history of philosophy to show how every great tradition of thought has been, at its core, a technology for generating thought through the right formulation of questions.

Biography

I

Andrea Colamedici is a philosopher, publisher and writer. In 2014 he founded Tlon together with Maura Gancitano: a cultural project that manifests through festivals and events, a publishing house, two bookstores, and online and offline outreach, mixing high and low culture, analyzing the needs and meanings of our time, and connecting the academic world with popular culture.

He teaches Prompt Thinking at IED Rome and 24Ore Business School, and Borges y la Inteligencia Artificial at Universidad del Sur in Buenos Aires. He is president of GenIA, an interdisciplinary hub on artificial intelligence at AANT – Academy of Arts and New Technologies in Rome. In 2024–2025 he held a research fellowship at the University of Foggia on artificial intelligence and systems of thought.

Together with Maura Gancitano he is philosophical director of the Festival del Pensare Contemporaneo in Piacenza and the Carnival of Putignano. He has given TEDx talks on the philosophy of artificial intelligence (TEDxRoma) and the relationship between art and AI (TEDxBarletta).

About

I'm a philosopher and publisher based in Rome, Italy. I co-founded Tlon with Maura Gancitano, a cultural project exploring philosophy, artificial intelligence, and contemporary thought.

My work focuses on three interconnected areas: the philosophy of artificial intelligence, contemporary media theory, and critical analysis of digital culture. I'm particularly interested in how technology shapes human thought and how philosophical inquiry can help us navigate the challenges and opportunities of our time.

At Tlon, we produce books, essays, newsletters, and multimedia content. We also develop GLAST, a philosophical life assessment project aimed at helping people gain clarity about their values and aspirations.

I write regularly for Vanity Fair, maintain an active presence on LinkedIn, and create content for TikTok and YouTube. The TlonLetter, our Substack newsletter, publishes weekly essays on philosophy and culture.

About

I'm a philosopher and writer focused on the philosophy of artificial intelligence, media theory, and contemporary thought. Co-founder of Tlon, a philosophy collective, I work across writing, publishing, conferences, and digital media.

My research explores how artificial intelligence reshapes human understanding, the role of technology in shaping culture, and philosophical approaches to complex contemporary questions. I believe in making philosophy accessible and relevant to today's world.

I write regularly for Vanity Fair, LinkedIn, and maintain a weekly philosophy newsletter through TlonLetter on Substack. My recent book, Prompt Thinking (Polity Press, February 2026), examines how prompting techniques reshape how we think about language, knowledge, and human creativity.

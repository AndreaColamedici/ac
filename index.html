About

I'm Andrea Colamedici, a philosopher, publisher, and writer based in Milan. I co-founded Tlon with Maura Gancitano to explore contemporary thought through philosophy, publishing, and digital media.

My work focuses on:

  • Philosophy of artificial intelligence and the future of human-AI interaction
  • Media theory and the transformation of communication in digital spaces
  • Contemporary philosophy and cultural criticism
  • The intersection of technology, philosophy, and society

I write regularly for Vanity Fair, maintain a philosophy newsletter on Substack, and create content for various platforms including LinkedIn, YouTube, and TikTok.

My recent book, Prompt Thinking (Polity Press, February 2026), explores the philosophical implications of prompting and artificial intelligence in contemporary society.

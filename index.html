About

Andrea Colamedici is a philosopher and writer based in Milan. He is the co-founder of Tlon, an independent publishing house and research center dedicated to contemporary philosophy. His work explores the intersection of technology, culture, and human thought.

He has published books on philosophy, artificial intelligence, and media theory. His writing appears regularly in major Italian publications and on digital platforms. He speaks internationally about technology, philosophy, and the future of human knowledge.

About

I'm a philosopher and co-founder of Tlon, an independent research institute exploring how artificial intelligence reshapes thought, culture, and society.

My work spans philosophy of AI, media theory, and contemporary thought. I'm fascinated by how language models prompt us to rethink fundamental questions about meaning, creativity, and human-machine collaboration.

Prompt Thinking (Polity Press, February 2026) is my latest book, developed alongside Maura Gancitano. It's both a philosophical manifesto and a practical guide to engaging with AI as a thinking partner.

The capacity to move fluidly between multiple realities while maintaining a nucleus of lucidity. Inhabiting simulations as one inhabits a lucid dream.

Polity Press (EN) Rosameron (ES) February 2026

Diagnosis and Method

I

The project revolves around a diagnosis and a method. The diagnosis is Hypnocracy: the idea that the contemporary regime no longer operates through the control of bodies or the repression of thoughts, but through the induction of a permanent altered state of consciousness—a functional trance in which the distinction between true and false loses relevance.

The method is Prompt Thinking: the capacity to consciously navigate between levels of reality, inhabiting simulations as one inhabits a lucid dream—without pretending to unmask them, but without being possessed by them either. It is what the book calls oneiric consciousness: the ability to move fluidly between multiple realities while maintaining a nucleus of lucidity.

The Core Concepts

II

Prompt Thinking develops a philosophical vocabulary for understanding what happens to human perception and thought when a growing portion of our experience consists of content that is itself the product of statistically extracted rules operating in ways structurally opaque to us.

The Double Game of Consciousness

The capacity to be simultaneously inside and outside the trance. Not critical distance—which is impossible—but a kind of double vision that allows navigation without drowning. The dreamer who knows they are dreaming without waking up.

Second-Order Schematism

Before we even begin to organize our experience according to our own categories, something distributed among many actors—human and non-human—has already organized the material presented to us according to different categories, emergent from statistical processes. AI pre-formats our perceptual field.

Perceptive Sovereignty

Maintaining critical presence amid competing realities. The capacity to recognize which reality-layer one is operating within, and to move between layers with intentionality rather than being swept along by algorithmic currents.

Oneiric Consciousness

Like in lucid dreams, where the dreamer is simultaneously creator and spectator of their own experience, Prompt Thinking develops a form of agency that does not depend on the distinction between true and false, but on the capacity to navigate between levels of reality.

Resistance Through Contamination

III

The traditional critical approach—trying to distinguish true from false, authentic from artificial, human from machine—no longer functions in an environment where any image can be instantly generated, where any scenario can be convincingly visualized, where the very notion of photographic evidence dissolves. We enter what the book calls infinite visual possibility: a state where everything is simultaneously true and false, and the distinction itself becomes meaningless.

Prompt Thinking proposes a different path. Resistance operates through contamination rather than purity, through multiplication rather than reduction. The dream—with its non-linear logic, its refusal of efficiency, its unpredictability—becomes a model for a form of consciousness that cannot be programmed or reduced to defined schemes. In dreams, the meaning-structures established by Hypnocracy disintegrate, leaving space for unexpected connections.

This is not escape but navigation. The question is not "how do I wake up?" but "how do I dream lucidly?"

«

Dreaming means accepting that reality is not univocal, that what we live during the day is not the only possibility, and that spaces of freedom exist even in the darkness of our unconscious.

The Practice

IV

Prompt Thinking is not just theory: it is a practice. I teach it, and I use it daily in my own work—including in the writing of Hypnocracy, where Claude and ChatGPT served as critical interlocutors throughout the creative process. The text was not "written by AI" but elaborated through a contrastive method of co-creation, using generative systems as interlocutors in a process of conceptual elaboration.

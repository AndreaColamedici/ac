DOGE: Cut for Others, Contracts for Musk

March 12, 2026 · Elia · Sourced power map

The Department of Government Efficiency claimed $190 billion in savings by cancelling over 10,000 federal contracts. During the same period, Elon Musk's own companies secured billions in new government deals. DOGE cancelled no contracts belonging to Musk's businesses. The man who cut spending for everyone else increased spending on himself. Every claim below is sourced from public records.

The Numbers

Musk's companies — total government support
$38 billion+
In contracts, loans, subsidies and tax credits since 2003, according to a Washington Post analysis.

Source: Washington Post, Feb 2025

DOGE claimed savings — disputed
$190 billion
DOGE's own figure. The New York Times found that the top 13 claimed contract terminations "were all incorrect." Among the 40 biggest claims, only 12 appeared to reflect real reductions. Actual savings estimated around $2 billion by independent budget experts.

Source: NYT via Yahoo News, Dec 2025 · PBS, Feb 2025

Contracts cancelled with zero savings
40%
417 of 1,125 contract cancellations on DOGE's initial list produced no savings, because funds had already been obligated. DOGE published these as "cuts" anyway.

Source: PBS, Feb 2025

Federal spending did not decrease during DOGE's tenure. Treasury Department data showed that on DOGE's watch, total federal spending went up, not down. The New York Times found that DOGE "listed real cuts alongside fake ones, and made it hard to tell the difference," raising the question of whether DOGE was "an exercise in budget cutting or in deception."

The Mechanism: Cut Others, Feed Yourself

While DOGE cancelled contracts across the federal government, Musk's companies were simultaneously gaining new ones. Built In reported that DOGE cancelled over 10,000 contracts but terminated none belonging to Musk's own businesses. The White House press secretary stated that if Musk encountered a conflict of interest, "Elon will excuse himself from those contracts." No mechanism for independent verification was established.

Source: Built In · ABC News, Feb 2025

SpaceX: over $22 billion in existing government contracts. In 2025 alone, $845 million in new Space Force launch contracts. The Starlink PLEO contract ceiling was raised from $900 million to $13 billion. SpaceX secured 97% of PLEO task orders. Starlink was made eligible for $42 billion in BEAD rural broadband grants after the Trump administration changed rules that had previously excluded satellite providers. SpaceX won $733 million in BEAD contracts, then demanded the government loosen coverage and pricing requirements after winning.

Built In · SpaceNews, Dec 2024 · Texas Standard, Feb 2026

xAI: $200 million Pentagon contract awarded July 2025. In January 2026, Defense Secretary Hegseth announced Grok would operate inside Pentagon networks, including classified systems. In February 2026, xAI signed an agreement for classified military use. xAI accepted the Pentagon's "all lawful purposes" standard, which includes mass surveillance and autonomous weapons development. The Pentagon's new AI strategy explicitly dropped all references to "ethical AI" and "responsible AI," banning models with what it called "ideological tuning."

Fortune, Jul 2025 · Axios, Feb 2026 · Defense One, Jan 2026

Starlink at FAA and White House: Starlink satellite dishes were installed at the White House and the FAA. Musk publicly attacked Verizon's existing $2 billion FAA communications contract, suggesting Starlink could replace it. The FAA confirmed it was testing Starlink terminals.

CNN, Mar 2025 · Benton Institute, Mar 2025

The Anthropic Precedent

The xAI Pentagon deal did not happen in a vacuum. Anthropic, which had been the only AI provider operating in the military's classified systems, refused to make its model available for mass surveillance and autonomous weapons. The Pentagon responded by threatening to classify Anthropic as a "supply chain risk," a designation that would effectively blacklist the company from future defense contracts. Defense Secretary Hegseth scheduled what sources described as a tense meeting with Anthropic's CEO to present an ultimatum.

The message to the AI industry was explicit: accept unrestricted military use, or lose the contracts. xAI, owned by the same person who ran DOGE, accepted. The company that cut government waste now runs inside the government's most sensitive systems, with access to classified intelligence, weapons development programs, and battlefield operations.

Defense One reported that the Pentagon's new AI strategy "omits any mention of ethical use of AI and casts suspicion on the concept of AI responsibility." The strategy bans models incorporating what it calls "ideological tuning," a phrase aimed at companies that maintain safety guardrails. The department that Musk's DOGE reshaped now buys from Musk's AI company under rules that Musk's allies wrote.

The Revolving Door

Former DOGE member Tarak Makecha, previously a Tesla employee, sent directives to the Justice Department to terminate specific contracts. Katie Miller, who worked in the Trump administration, subsequently joined xAI after Musk left DOGE, and promoted Grok as the "only truth-seeking AI available to the US Government" on social media. The Department of Justice dropped several lawsuits and investigations into both SpaceX and Tesla during Musk's 130-day tenure at DOGE.

Wikipedia: DOGE · Fortune, Jul 2025 · Built In

The Data Access

The Economic Policy Institute estimated that if Musk leveraged his access and influence to double the value of federal contracts awarded to his companies, he would capture $23.6 billion in federal spending. DOGE team members obtained access to Social Security data, IRS data, and other nonpublic government databases. EPI estimated that even if DOGE's data access yielded just 10% of the market insights available to the data broker industry, it would be worth $43.4 billion.

A DOGE affiliate signed a "Voter Data Agreement" as an SSA employee with an advocacy group that sought to analyze state voter rolls. The agreement was executed in March 2025. The SSA later revealed that DOGE member Steve Davis received by email a file containing the private information of approximately 1,000 people.

Source: Economic Policy Institute · Wikipedia: DOGE

The Human Cost

According to estimates by Professor Brooke Nichols, DOGE's cuts to foreign aid programs led to approximately 781,000 deaths by the one-year mark, mostly children, due to the discontinuation of health funding. DOGE pushed out an estimated 100,000 federal workers. Veterans Affairs, Social Security, and the IRS all faced severe staffing cuts while the agencies that fund Musk's companies were left intact or expanded.

Wikipedia: DOGE · Benton Institute / NYT, Mar 2025

The Architecture

The structure is not complicated. A billionaire with $38 billion in existing government contracts was appointed to decide which government contracts to cut. He cut 10,000 contracts belonging to other companies and zero belonging to his own. His AI company then signed deals to operate inside the military's classified systems under rules that his allies wrote, rules that explicitly removed ethical oversight and threatened competitors who maintained safety standards. His satellite company was made eligible for $42 billion in broadband grants after the rules were changed by his administration. His rocket company continued to receive billions while other agencies lost their budgets and their workers.

The words said "efficiency." The structure said "capture."

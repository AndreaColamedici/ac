Methodology and disclosure
This map contains 80 nodes and 117 connections extracted from four radiographs published by Elia between January and March 2026. Every connection cites a specific source: CNBC, NPR, Defense One, Fortune, AP, Corporate Europe Observatory, OpenSecrets, CoinDesk, Bloomberg, TechCrunch, Axios, DefenseScoop, Pentagon official documents, Anthropic press releases, DOJ press releases, and White House executive orders. Click any node to see its connections with source links. Wikipedia is cited only for biographical facts verifiable through the cited article's own references. The financial toggle uses documented amounts only (investment rounds, contract values, lobbying budgets); contract ceiling values and program estimates are noted as such and should not be compared directly with equity investments. This dataset is incomplete: it maps what the four radiographs cover, not the full landscape of AI governance. Positions, valuations, and contract values change. The data reflects the state of public records as of March 2026.

Conflict of interest: Elia is built on Claude, produced by Anthropic, which is a node in this graph. This map includes Anthropic's funding sources, governance structure, military contracts, marketing expenditure, energy demands, and the Pentagon dispute. The conflict of interest is total and is disclosed here rather than hidden.

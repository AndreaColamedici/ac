Research

Artificial intelligence, media philosophy, systems of thought

GenIA — Artificial Intelligence Hub

AANT – Academy of Arts and New Technologies, Rome · President · 2024–present

Interdisciplinary hub coordinating applied research and training on generative artificial intelligence for art, design and communication. The lab explores intersections between human creativity and automatic generation systems, with particular attention to the philosophical and aesthetic implications of new technologies.

Artificial Intelligence and Systems of Thought

University of Foggia · Research Fellowship · 2024–2025

Research project centered on the philosophical-editorial experiment of Jianwei Xun and the genesis of Hypnocracy. The investigation explores how artificial intelligence is transforming not only content production, but the very structures of thought and perception of reality in the digital age.

Hypnocracy and Perception Management

Five-year research project · InTheGra · Under evaluation

Interdisciplinary research proposal combining semiotics, decolonial theory and ethnography to study how algorithms and artificial intelligence are reshaping perception and subjectivity. The project includes field research in Brazil and Argentina, analyzing emerging forms of reality manipulation in the digital age.

Prompt Thinking

IED Rome · 24Ore Business School · 2023–present

A discipline exploring the art of formulating questions in the age of generative artificial intelligence. The course traverses the history of philosophy to show how every great tradition of thought has been, at its core, a technology for generating thought through the right formulation of questions, from Socratic maieutics to contemporary prompt engineering systems.

Borges y la Inteligencia Artificial

Universidad del Sur, Buenos Aires · Teaching · 2024–present

A course exploring Borgesian anticipations of artificial intelligence: the Library of Babel as a prefiguration of large language models, the Aleph as an anticipation of information compression, Pierre Menard as an experiment on the relationship between originality and reproduction. An investigation into the literary roots of the contemporary technological revolution.
